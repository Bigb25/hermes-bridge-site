Hermes Google Workspace

A personal assistant application that helps its owner organize his own email and calendar.

What this application does

Hermes Google Workspace is a private, single-user application that runs on the owner's own home server as part of his personal assistant software ("Hermes"). It connects to the owner's own Google account so the assistant can help manage his email and schedule: it reads his email to learn his patterns and organize his mailbox, and reads his calendar so the assistant knows his schedule.

The application currently uses read-only Google permissions: it can view email messages and settings in the owner's Gmail, and see and download the calendars the owner can access in Google Calendar. It cannot send, modify, or delete email or calendar data under these permissions.

Who uses it

This is not a public service. It has no sign-up, no accounts for other people, and no advertising. It is operated by one individual, for his own email and calendar, on his own hardware.

Privacy

How the application accesses, uses, and stores Google user data is described in the Privacy Policy.
