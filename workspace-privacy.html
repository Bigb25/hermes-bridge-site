Privacy Policy — Hermes Google Workspace

Effective October 16, 2026

Overview

Hermes Google Workspace is a personal, single-user application that runs on the owner's own home server as part of his personal assistant software. It accesses the owner's own Google account to help organize his email and to read his calendar.

Data the application accesses

The application requests read-only Google permissions:

  • Gmail (read-only) — view the owner's email messages and settings (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly), so the assistant can learn the owner's email patterns and help organize his mailbox.
  • Google Calendar (read-only) — see and download calendars the owner can access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly), so the assistant knows the owner's schedule.

The application also receives the owner's email address as part of the standard Google sign-in, used only to identify the connected account. Under these permissions the application cannot send, modify, or delete the owner's email or calendar data.

How the data is used

Accessed data is used solely to operate the owner's personal assistant: understanding his email so it can be organized the way he prefers, and being aware of his calendar. No data is used for advertising, profiling for third parties, or any purpose unrelated to running the owner's own assistant.

Storage

Data is processed and stored on the owner's own home server, under his control. The application stores its Google sign-in token on that server so the connection persists. The developer operates no external or cloud server that receives or stores the owner's Google data.

Sharing

Data accessed through Google APIs is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Limited Use disclosure

Hermes Google Workspace's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Any stored data remains on the owner's server under his control and can be deleted by him at any time. The owner can revoke the application's access at any time from the third-party access page of his Google Account, which immediately ends the application's ability to reach his email and calendar.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If the application is ever granted broader permissions, this policy will be updated first to describe them.
